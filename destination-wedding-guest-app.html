Why destination weddings are harder

More distance, more moving parts.

A wedding at home, guests can improvise. A wedding away from home, they can't. Flights land at odd hours and need pickups. Hotel rooms have to be assigned and communicated. Venues, airlines and visas ask for government IDs in advance. And half the party may be NRI guests coordinating across timezones. That's a lot to run over WhatsApp and a spreadsheet — which is exactly where destination weddings go wrong.

Questions

Destination weddings, answered.

What is a destination wedding guest app?

It's a private app that gives out-of-town and overseas guests everything they need for a wedding away from home — the schedule, travel and arrival details, their hotel room, directions, and secure ID upload — so a wedding in another city or country runs without a hundred phone calls.

How does Baraati help with guest travel?

Guests enter their flight or train details in the app; hosts see a single arrivals board and get suggested shared pickups that cluster guests landing together into one vehicle. Guests also see their assigned hotel room in-app.

Can I collect guest IDs securely for hotels and visas?

Yes. Baraati collects government IDs in an encrypted vault using AES-256 envelope encryption. IDs are never served over public links, every access is logged, and documents are automatically deleted after the wedding — far safer than photos of passports over WhatsApp. How it's secured →

Does it work for NRI guests travelling from abroad?

Yes. Guests sign in with a one-time code to their email or phone from anywhere, see the full itinerary, share international flight details, and upload the documents a venue or airline needs — all before they land.
