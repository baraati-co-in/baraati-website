What it means

What is wedding guest management?

Wedding guest management is everything the hosts do to organise the people at a wedding — building the guest list, grouping guests by side or event, tracking who's coming, assigning hotel rooms, collecting travel details and any documents venues need, and keeping everyone informed. At an Indian wedding, that's hundreds of people over several days. Baraati brings all of it into one app, so the host side finally has a single source of truth instead of a spreadsheet nobody trusts and a WhatsApp group nobody reads.

Sensitive data, handled properly

Guest information stays private.

Managing guests means holding sensitive details — contact numbers, travel plans, and sometimes the government IDs a venue, hotel or airline requires. Baraati is built so none of it leaks. Every wedding is invite-only and nothing is public or searchable. Government ID documents are protected with AES-256 envelope encryption, are never served over public links, are access-logged each time they're opened, and are automatically deleted after the wedding. Questions

Guest management, answered.

What is wedding guest management?

Wedding guest management is everything the hosts do to organise the people at a wedding — building the guest list, grouping guests, tracking RSVPs, assigning hotel rooms, collecting travel details and any documents venues need, and communicating with everyone. Baraati brings all of it into one app instead of spreadsheets and WhatsApp.

How do I manage RSVPs in Baraati?

Guests RSVP by sliding on their invitation, and hosts see a live headcount of yes, no, tentative and pending across the whole wedding, with a notification for every reply and the ability to nudge anyone still pending.

Can I organise guests into groups?

Yes. Segment guests into groups — bride's side, bus 2, college friends — and message a single group precisely instead of blasting the whole list.

How does Baraati handle hotel rooms and travel?

Hosts assign each guest a hotel room they see in-app, and guests enter their own flight and train details so hosts get a single arrivals board with suggested shared pickups.

Is guest information kept private?

Yes. Every wedding is invite-only, and no guest information is public or searchable. Government ID documents are AES-256 encrypted, access-logged and auto-deleted after the wedding, so sensitive details are never exposed.
