Refund, Cancellation & Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026  ·  Effective: 1 August 2026

This policy explains how cancellations, refunds and delivery work for paid plans on Baraati, operated by Baraati LLP ("Baraati", "we", "us"). It forms part of our Terms of Use. If anything here is unclear, please write to us before you pay — we would much rather answer a question than process a refund.

1. What you are paying for

Baraati plans are a one-time, prepaid purchase made per wedding — not a subscription. There is no auto-renewal and no recurring mandate on your card or UPI.

  • Free — ₹0, up to 10 guests. Nothing is charged, so nothing is refundable.
  • Standard — ₹11,000. Unlimited guests, plus 150 complimentary printed A5 thank-you cards.
  • Premium — ₹21,000. Everything in Standard, plus a published post-wedding album website hosted for 3 years.

All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise at checkout. We sell and deliver within India only.

2. Cooling-off window — full refund within 3 days

You may cancel your plan and receive a full refund within 3 days (72 hours) of payment, provided that none of the following has happened yet:

  • your post-wedding album has been published;
  • your thank-you card order has been submitted for printing (i.e. it has moved beyond draft);
  • the first day of your wedding has already begun.

If none of those apply, you get 100% of what you paid back — no questions, no deduction.

3. After the cooling-off window

Once any of the three events above occurs, or once 3 days have passed, the purchase is non-refundable. This is because the value of the plan is delivered at that point and cannot be returned to us:

  • Guest capacity is unlocked immediately on payment — your invited guests can join, RSVP, and upload photos straight away.
  • A published album is a hosted website with its own public link that we commit to keeping live for 3 years; once published and shared with your guests, it cannot be un-delivered.
  • Printed thank-you cards are personalised to your wedding and produced to order, so they cannot be restocked or resold.
  • Your wedding is a fixed, dated event — capacity we reserved for it cannot be resold afterwards.

We do not offer partial or pro-rata refunds for features you chose not to use, for guests who did not join, or for a wedding that is postponed or cancelled after the cooling-off window. If your wedding is postponed, contact us — we will do our best to move your plan to the new dates at no extra cost, which is almost always the better outcome for you.

4. Album renewal

A Premium album is hosted for 3 years from the date it is published. After that it may be renewed for ₹510 per year. Renewal is optional and is never charged automatically — if you do nothing, the album simply expires. Renewal payments are non-refundable once the renewed hosting period has begun.

5. Failed, duplicate or unrecognised payments

If money was debited but your plan did not activate, please do not pay again — write to us with the payment reference and we will resolve it. Genuine duplicate charges and failed transactions are refunded in full.

  • Payments that fail at the bank are typically auto-reversed by your bank within 5–7 business days without us needing to do anything.
  • Duplicate or mis-charged payments confirmed by us are refunded in full within 7 business days of confirmation.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@baraati.co.in from the email address on your Baraati account, including:

  • the couple's names / your wedding name as it appears in the app;
  • the Razorpay payment ID or order ID from your payment confirmation email;
  • the reason for the request (optional, but it helps us improve).

We acknowledge every request within 2 business days and confirm our decision within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method — we cannot refund to a different card, account or UPI ID.

7. Refund timelines

Once we approve and initiate a refund through our payment partner, the money typically reaches you within 5–7 business days. The exact time depends on your bank or card issuer and is outside our control; UPI refunds are usually fastest. You will receive confirmation from Razorpay when the refund is initiated.

8. Delivery & shipping

Digital features

Your plan is activated immediately on successful payment — usually within a minute, and always within 24 hours. There is nothing to ship and no delivery charge. If your plan has not activated within 24 hours, contact us and we will fix it or refund you in full.

Printed thank-you cards

Standard and Premium include 150 complimentary printed A5 thank-you cards. These are produced only after you submit your design and shipping address in the app, so the timeline starts from your submission — not from your payment date.

  • Cards are dispatched within 10 business days of you submitting your design and confirmed shipping address.
  • Delivery within India typically takes a further 5–7 business days depending on your location.
  • Shipping is free — there are no delivery charges, and we ship within India only.
  • You may edit your design and address freely while the order is still a draft; once submitted for printing it can no longer be changed or cancelled.

If your cards arrive damaged in transit or materially differ from the design you approved, tell us within 7 days of delivery with photographs and we will reprint and reship them at no cost to you.
