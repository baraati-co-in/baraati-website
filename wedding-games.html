Why games

The fastest way to warm up a room of 300.

At a big Indian wedding, half the guests have never met the other half. Games fix that faster than any welcome speech — they give people a reason to talk, get everyone capturing the moments you'd have missed, and keep the energy up between ceremonies. Baraati's games are built right into the wedding app, so there's nothing extra to download; hosts simply unlock each one on cue — trivia at the sangeet, the scavenger hunt running all week.

Questions

Wedding games, answered.

What wedding games does Baraati have?

Baraati includes I-Spy (a photo scavenger hunt), a Word Search packed with facts about the couple, Predictions (live polls), and Most-Likely-To (bride or groom). Points from every game feed a single live leaderboard the couple calls Shaadi Ka Khel.

Do guests need a separate app to play?

No. The games are built into the same Baraati app your guests already use for the invitation, schedule and photos — so there's nothing extra to download and everyone can join in.

How do wedding games help at a big wedding?

At a large Indian wedding many guests are strangers to each other. Games give people a reason to talk, get them capturing the moments you would have missed, and turn a big room into one celebration. Hosts unlock each game on cue — trivia at the sangeet, I-Spy through the week.

Is the photo scavenger hunt private?

Yes. I-Spy photos go into your wedding's private feed, visible only to your guests — no strangers and no public sharing.
