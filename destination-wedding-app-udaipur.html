Why Udaipur is its own challenge

Beautiful across the lake — and spread across it too.

Udaipur is India's most-loved wedding destination for a reason: sunset ceremonies on the water, palace courtyards, and a skyline nothing else matches. But the same things that make it magical make it hard to run. Venues sit apart around Lake Pichola and Fateh Sagar, guests arrive on flights spread across the day into Maharana Pratap Airport, and the heritage hotels and palace properties usually need guest IDs before check-in.

That's a lot of coordination for a family WhatsApp group. Baraati holds all of it in one private app — so your Udaipur guests always know which venue, which room, and which car, without a single "where do I go?" call.
