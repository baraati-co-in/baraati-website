Questions

Digital wedding invitations, answered.

What is a digital wedding invitation with RSVP?

It's an online wedding invitation your guests open on their phone instead of a paper card or PDF. In Baraati, guests open a full-screen animated invitation and respond by sliding to confirm — Yes, Can't make it, or Tentative — and you get their reply instantly.

How do guests RSVP to a Baraati invitation?

Guests open the invitation, read your details, and slide to confirm. You see a live headcount of yes, no, tentative and pending, and get a push notification for every response — no chasing replies in the family WhatsApp group.

Is the digital wedding invitation free?

Yes. Baraati is free for a wedding with up to 10 guests, and guests always join free on every plan. Paid plans unlock unlimited guests, printed thank-you cards, and a published album website. See pricing →

Can I include multiple ceremonies in one invitation?

Yes. Baraati is built for multi-day Indian weddings. Your invitation lays out every ceremony — mehndi, haldi, sangeet, baraat, phera, reception — each with its own date, time and venue.

How do I share the invitation with my guests?

You share one private invite link. Guests tap it, join your wedding, and land straight on your animated invitation — no app-store hunting, no adding 300 numbers to a group.
