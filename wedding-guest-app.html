The basics

What is a wedding guest app?

A wedding guest app is a private, invite-only app built around a single wedding — and, crucially, around the people attending it. Instead of the couple broadcasting details across WhatsApp groups and email forwards, every guest gets one place that holds the invitation and RSVP, the multi-day schedule, venues and directions, travel and hotel details, a shared photo feed, and games to play through the week.

It is not a wedding-planning app. Planning apps help the couple find vendors, track budgets, and manage checklists before the wedding. A wedding guest app is for the wedding itself — the days when 300 people need to know where to be, what to wear, how to get there, and where they're staying. Baraati is that app, built for the shape of an Indian wedding.

Questions

Wedding guest apps, answered.

What is a wedding guest app?

A wedding guest app is a private app your guests use for one wedding — it holds the invitation and RSVP, the full multi-day schedule, venues and directions, travel and hotel details, a photo feed and games, so guests have everything in one place instead of scattered WhatsApp groups and PDFs.

How is it different from a wedding planning app?

A wedding planning app helps you find vendors and manage budgets before the wedding. A wedding guest app like Baraati is for the wedding itself and for your guests — invitation, RSVP, schedule, travel, photos and games during the celebration.

Do guests have to pay or create an account?

No. Guests always join free on every plan. They tap your invite link, sign in with a one-time code — no password — and they're in. Baraati is free for a wedding with up to 10 guests; paid plans unlock unlimited guests. See pricing →

Is it suitable for a multi-day Indian wedding?

Yes — that's exactly what Baraati is built for. Every ceremony from mehndi to vidaai gets its own schedule, venue and dress code, and hosts can manage travel, hotel rooms and guest IDs for the whole week.
