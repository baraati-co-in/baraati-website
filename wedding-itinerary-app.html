Questions

Wedding itineraries, answered.

What is a wedding itinerary app?

A wedding itinerary app puts your full multi-day schedule in every guest's pocket — each ceremony with its date, time, venue, directions and dress code — and keeps it current, so guests always know where to be.

Does it handle multi-day Indian weddings?

Yes. Baraati is built for multi-day weddings — mehndi, haldi, sangeet, baraat, phere and reception each get their own card with venue, time and dress code, across as many days as your celebration runs. More on the guest app →

What happens when a timing or venue changes?

Update it once and every guest's schedule refreshes instantly, with a push notification — no "please note the revised timing" forwards in the family group.

Can guests get directions to each venue?

Yes. Each ceremony carries its venue with a map link, so guests can navigate straight there, and hosts can broadcast live updates like shuttle timings.
