Why Goa is its own challenge

Laid-back for guests — a puzzle for hosts.

Goa is the beach-wedding capital of India: barefoot ceremonies as the sun drops into the Arabian Sea, resort lawns strung with lights, and a party that runs later than anywhere. But the logistics are deceptively tricky — venues stretch from Candolim and Anjuna in the north to Cavelossim in the south, guests arrive into two different airports (Dabolim and the newer Mopa), and every resort wants IDs at check-in.

All of that over a WhatsApp group means someone spends the trip answering "which beach again?" Baraati holds it in one private app, so your guests always know which venue, which room, and which car — and can get back to the susegad.

Questions

Goa weddings, answered.

How does Baraati help with a wedding in Goa?

Baraati gives every guest one private app for your Goa wedding — the full schedule across your beach and resort venues, their room, their airport pickup from Goa's Dabolim or Mopa airport, secure ID upload for resorts, and a shared photo feed for the week.

Guests fly into two Goa airports — can the app manage arrivals?

Yes. Guests enter their flights into Goa's Dabolim (GOI) or Manohar/Mopa (GOX) airport, and hosts see one arrivals board with suggested shared pickups that group guests landing close together into a single vehicle.

Do Goa resorts need guest IDs?

Beach resorts and hotels in Goa commonly require guest IDs at check-in. Baraati collects them in an encrypted vault — AES-256 encryption, access-logged, and auto-deleted after the wedding — instead of over WhatsApp. How it's secured →
