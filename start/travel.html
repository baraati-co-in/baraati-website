Quick answers
Do my guests need to download anything?

No. Guests open your invite link in their phone's browser, reply, add their travel and see their room there. Only you, the host, use the app to set up the wedding.

How are the shared pickups planned?

From the arrival times guests enter. Baraati groups guests landing at the same station or airport around the same time into a suggested pickup, and you decide who goes in which car.

Is it really free?

Yes, for a wedding with up to 10 guests, with no card needed. For a bigger guest list there are paid plans with unlimited guests. Guests always join free.

Our guests are coming from abroad. Does that work?

Yes. Guests can sign in with a one-time code by email from anywhere, or by text to phone numbers in India and many other countries, including the US, UK, UAE, Canada and Australia.
