Questions

Wedding photo sharing, answered.

What is a wedding photo sharing app?

A wedding photo sharing app gives everyone at your wedding one private place to post and see photos and videos. In Baraati the feed is invite-only — visible to your guests, not to strangers or a public algorithm.

Is the wedding photo feed private?

Yes. The feed belongs to one wedding and is only visible to that wedding's members. Baraati has no ads, no public sharing, and no behavioural tracking, so your photos stay among the people who were there. How it's secured →

Do guests need to make an account to share photos?

No account or password — guests join with your invite link and a one-time code, then post to the feed straight away. It's designed for guests of every age.

What happens to the photos after the wedding?

On the Premium plan, your wedding is published as an album website that keeps every feed photo online for years at your own address, so you can share one link with everyone who was there. See pricing →
