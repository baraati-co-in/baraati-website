    Questions

    RSVP tracking, answered.

    How do I track wedding RSVPs for free?

    Use this free wedding RSVP tracker: add each guest and how many people are in their party, then mark them as yes, no or maybe. The tool keeps a live headcount for you. It runs in your browser with no sign-up.

    Is my guest list private?

    Yes. This tool saves your guest list only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server and there's no account, so your list stays with you.

    Can my guests RSVP themselves?

    Not in this simple tracker — you enter replies yourself. If you want guests to RSVP on their own by sliding on a digital invitation, and to get a live headcount automatically, use the Baraati wedding app.
